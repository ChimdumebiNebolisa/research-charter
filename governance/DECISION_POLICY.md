# Decision Policy

After Phase 2 starts, operation is a tournament among the three fixed problems, not three permanent equal-priority projects.

Periodic research-director reviews must answer:

1. What new information was obtained?
2. Which hypotheses were falsified?
3. What moved on the true objective?
4. What apparent progress failed verification?
5. Are experiments becoming repetitive?
6. Is compute producing information?
7. What is the expected value of another experiment on each problem?
8. Which problem deserves the next unit of compute?
9. Does any problem meet a kill, pause, promote, or commit condition?

The eventual operating point should be one primary direction with at most one cheap hedge. A problem may be paused or killed for low information yield, repeated failure, cost, or unresolved foundations; that does not change the research question. A direction may be promoted when it produces reproducible movement on the true objective and survives verification.

`state/SCOREBOARD.md` is a derived summary only. The experiment records, raw artifacts, validation outputs, and append-only logs are the scientific source of truth.
